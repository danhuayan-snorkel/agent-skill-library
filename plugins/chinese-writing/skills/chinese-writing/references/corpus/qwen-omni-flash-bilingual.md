## What to avoid (drift found in the pair)

- **One concept named two ways.** Adjacent Chinese sentences say 1M 长上下文 and 1M 长序列 for the same feature. The English consistently says 1M-token context window.
- **Heavy English retention for a broad audience.** The Chinese keeps Agent, Agentic, Harness, Caption, Token, Coding, and Static/Agentic Understanding, and the heading 可控音视频 Caption mixes languages. That suits developers. For general business readers, prefer 字幕描述 or 视频描述, and 智能体 where appropriate.
- **Business jargon.** 沉淀, 文档资产, 内容资产, and 能力边界 are common in Chinese tech marketing. Use them only in a matching register. The English replaces them with plain verbs.
- **Unqualified "first" claims.** 首个支持"听声辨位"的全模态大模型 / "the first omnimodal model capable of locating targets by sound" is a marketing claim. Do not repeat "first" or "best" claims in neutral writing without a source and scope.
