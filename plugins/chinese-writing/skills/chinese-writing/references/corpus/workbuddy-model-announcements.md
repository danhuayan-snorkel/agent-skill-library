## Tentative observations

A short announcement names the change first, then describes intended work and relevant capabilities. The update card connects operational changes (time and output length) with user benefits (speed and cost). Repeated subjects can stay implicit within that chain.

English names and terms such as Office, Agentic, and model identifiers sit naturally inside Chinese syntax. This is evidence that forced translation is not always necessary, not a rule to retain all English. The phrase 原生多模态 is useful terminology evidence within a model-capability description. 模型接入 refers to availability/integration in a product, not training or model alignment.
